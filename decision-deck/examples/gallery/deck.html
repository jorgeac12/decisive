Example · Fictional company Northwind Logistics

Executive summary

Consolidating three warehouses cuts delivery cost only if two owner decisions come first

The problem

1

Duplicate stock

The same items are held in three sites, tying up working capital.

2

Slow routing

Orders are routed by hand, adding a day to most deliveries.

3

No single owner

Each site reports its own numbers with its own definitions.

The way out

4

One hub, one owner

A single regional hub with a named operations owner.

5

Automated routing

Rules decide the route; people handle exceptions.

6

One metric book

Cost per delivery defined once, reported weekly.

Two decisions are requested at the end. The most urgent: name the hub owner.

SCQA: situation, complication, question, answer. The answer is up front.
